Latch modelled off a Vessels hatch.Designed to have Clip at bottom and Latch above which can then be locked around the ClipCan be locked with Padlock.Hinged using two M3 20mm bolts and corresponding Locknuts.Latch can be printed as whole with Clip Separate.Individual latch parts can also be printed separately if required
